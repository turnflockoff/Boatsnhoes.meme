FAQ

Can I buy $BNH?

No. It doesn't exist yet. When it launches, the plan is a fair launch you mine, not a presale.

Is this Ethereum mining?

No. Ethereum switched to proof of stake in 2022 and can't be mined. $BNH is planned as its own proof-of-work chain.

Why the hoe?

It's a garden hoe. You mine with it. The boat holds the hoe. Please don't overthink the boat.

How can I help right now?

Run the 10-second GPU check and the Linux commands on the contributor page, then join the tester list.

Is it an investment?

No. It's a meme coin project in draft. Nothing here is financial advice, and there are no promised returns, payouts, or prices.
