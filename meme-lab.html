Using the art

Keep it funny. Keep it honest.

Go for it

Yes, please

  • Memes, fan art, stickers and banners about the project.
  • Jokes about boats, GPUs, garden hoes and the crew.
  • Remixing the boat and the colours.
Not with our art

Please don't

  • Price, profit or pump talk. No coin exists, and we won't post it.
  • Pretending to be official, or announcing tokens, airdrops or giveaways.
  • Selling merch or anything else as if it were ours.
  • Hate, harassment, explicit content, or real people who didn't agree to it.
  • Other people's art or characters you don't have the rights to.
Community gallery

Send in your best.

Every meme is looked at by a person before it's posted, so it can take a while, and not everything gets in. Posted memes appear in the gallery below and on GitHub, credited with the name you give.

  • One picture, up to 8 MB (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP).
  • Photos can carry hidden details, like the location where they were taken. Those are removed before a meme is posted, and memes made with the maker above never have them.
  • Your email is optional and only used if we need to ask you something about your meme.
  • No coins, points or prizes for memes. Just glory.
