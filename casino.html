Before a single game

What has to happen first.

Gambling is one of the most regulated things you can do online. Each of these is a gate: if one can't be passed, the casino doesn't happen.

  1. Pick where it's legal, and get licensedChoose a jurisdiction and a licence route with a gambling lawyer. Players in places where it isn't allowed would be blocked.
  2. Legal review of the coin itselfUsing a coin in a casino raises securities, money-transmission and tax questions. These get answered before launch, not after.
  3. Age and identity checksLegal age only (18+, or older where the law says so), with real age verification and the identity and anti-money-laundering checks a licence requires.
  4. Provably fair games, independently auditedEvery game's randomness checkable by the player, and the games and code reviewed by an outside auditor.
  5. Security for players' fundsMost funds kept offline, withdrawal limits and delays, and more confirmations for bigger deposits. Planned, not built.
  6. Responsible-gambling tools working on day oneLimits, time-outs and self-exclusion before the first bet, not added later.

If gambling stops being fun

Help is free.

If you or someone you know is gambling more than they want to, free help is available. In the US, the National Council on Problem Gambling lists helplines and treatment at ncpgambling.org. Elsewhere, search for your country's gambling helpline.
